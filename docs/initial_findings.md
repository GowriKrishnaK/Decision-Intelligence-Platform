# Retail Profit Intelligence — Initial Findings

## 1. Business Question
Which products and categories should management investigate to improve profitability?

## 2. Data Quality
- Duplicate order IDs: None found.
- Blank cells in the original data: None found.
- Discount values: Within the expected range.
- Calculated metrics: Gross sales, net sales, profit, and profit margin.

## 3. Initial Findings
- Technology has the highest total net sales.
- Office Supplies has the lowest total profit.
- Furniture has the lowest overall profit margin.
- Desk has lower total profit than Chair in the Furniture data.
- Desk profit per unit differs considerably across the observed discount levels.

## 4. Recommendation
Review high discounts on Desks and test whether smaller discounts can maintain demand while improving profit.

## 5. Limitations
- The dataset contains only 12 order records.
- The observations do not prove that discounts caused lower profitability.
- Profit is calculated after product cost and discount, excluding overheads, taxes, shipping, and other operating expenses.
- Findings should be validated using a larger dataset before business decisions are made.
